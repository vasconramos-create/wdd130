White Water Rafting Company

Our purpose is to provide thrilling, safe, and unforgettable white water rafting experiences for individuals, families, and groups. We are committed to preserving the natural beauty of our rivers while creating lasting memories for our guests.

History

Founded in 1995, our company began with just two rafts and a passion for the river. Over the decades, we have grown into a premier rafting outfitter, guiding thousands of adventurers through some of the most exciting rapids in the region.
